# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming and inclusive environment for all contributors and participants in this project, regardless of background or identity.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive in all interactions
- Listen actively and consider different perspectives
- Provide and receive feedback gracefully
- Focus on what is best for the community

## Unacceptable Behavior

Harassment, discrimination, or any form of disrespectful conduct is not tolerated.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please contact the maintainers directly.
