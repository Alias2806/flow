# Contributing

Thank you for your interest in contributing to the flow repository!

## How to Contribute

1. **Fork** the repository
2. **Create a branch** for your idea or documentation
3. **Document your decision** or starter template clearly
4. **Submit a pull request** with context and reasoning
5. **Participate in review** to refine and validate the idea

## Guidelines

- Clearly explain the decision or idea and its rationale
- Include any relevant context or alternatives considered
- Be open to feedback and discussion
- Ensure your contribution aligns with the project's purpose

## Before Graduation

Ideas in this repo should be tested and validated before proposing graduation to a dedicated repository.
